Christmas Facts

Space

A song among the stars

The first song ever played in space was "Jingle Bells" — performed by the Gemini 6 astronauts in 1965 with a tiny harmonica and sleigh bells.

Giant

A 221-foot tree

The tallest cut Christmas tree ever displayed stood 221 feet tall — a Douglas fir put up in Seattle in 1950.

Bright Idea

Lights, 1882

Edward Johnson, a colleague of Thomas Edison, created the first string of electric Christmas lights in 1882 — 80 red, white and blue bulbs.

Tradition

A tree for London

Norway has sent a Christmas tree to London every year since 1947, as thanks for Britain's help in the Second World War. It stands in Trafalgar Square.
